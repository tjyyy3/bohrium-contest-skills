# Provenance and boundaries

This candidate is independently written for template adaptation. No external script or skill text is vendored or executed. External research originals and their licenses are stored outside the candidate in the task's `research/` directory. This candidate does not depend on them at runtime.

Useful lessons include preserving source object relationships when editing, retaining native evidence objects, planning the talk around source-backed content, and inspecting actual renders. This candidate chooses template inheritance over a new palette or fresh master. It does not adopt global installation instructions, compulsory decoration, automatic backup pages or a claim that a font list is universally safe. Font availability is measured on the actual host.

Local demonstration data and templates are synthetic and remain outside the candidate. They show an interface and test behavior; they are not official cases, scoring references or evidence of an improvement over the organizer's baseline. Formal evaluation and deployment remain unverified.
